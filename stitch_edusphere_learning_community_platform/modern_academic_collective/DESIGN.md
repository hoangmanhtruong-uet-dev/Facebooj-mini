## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an elevated, contemporary digital environment tailored for collaborative scholarship and peer-driven learning. It bridges academic rigor with social accessibility—stripping away legacy classroom austerity while rejecting chaotic social media noise. 

The interface evokes a sense of intellectual clarity, vibrant curiosity, and structural trust. Interactions feel crisp, responsive, and purposeful. The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** structure with **Soft Minimalist** warmth: high-legibility typographic hierarchies, expansive surface areas, and finely calibrated structural boundaries that encourage sustained reading, dynamic group discourse, and peer recognition.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on high-contrast functionality paired with intentional chromatic accents to steer focus across learning modules and social feeds.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` Indigo & `#312E81` Deep Slate):** Anchors core actions, navigation bars, primary calls-to-action, and active study thread indicators. Deep Slate serves as an alternative high-authority tone for course headers and persistent sidebars.
- **Secondary (`#06B6D4` Cyan/Teal):** Used sparingly for progress tracks, interactive real-time badges (e.g., active study rooms, live polls), and secondary actions requiring an energetic, optimistic presence.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Amber):** Reserved for recognition, achievements, system highlights, and role statuses requiring clear differentiation.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Background Canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for reduced eye strain across prolonged reading.
  - Surface Elevated: `#FFFFFF` for content cards, popovers, and side-panels.
  - Borders & Dividers: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) providing soft, non-distracting containment.
  - Typographic Hierarchy: `#0F172A` (Headlines/Strong text), `#334155` (Body content), and `#64748B` (Subtext, metadata, and timestamps).

## Typography
Typographic discipline prioritizes structural legibility across bilingual contexts, long-form discussion threads, and code-mixed educational materials. 

- **Headlines & Body (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Engineered for exceptional clarity across complex diacritics and bilingual scripts. Its open apertures and balanced proportions maintain rhythm in multi-paragraph lesson posts, comments, and discussions.
- **Micro-Copy & Data Labels (`Inter`):** Applied to role tags, navigation nodes, timestamps, metrics, and tabular information. Inter offers high structural stability at small scales, maximizing legibility in crowded community feeds and dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a flexible 12-column responsive grid system based on an 8px base grid rhythm (with a 4px sub-grid for precise micro-alignments).

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max layout width capped at 1440px. Uses a standard 3-column operational layout: Sticky contextual navigation (left, 240px–280px), Primary feed or lecture module (center, max 720px), and Community widgets / Live channels (right, 320px). Gutters set at `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout. The tertiary right-hand widget sidebar collapses into drawer overlays; primary feed expands to span the main viewport.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` (`16px`) outer margins and gutters. Global navigation shifts to an elevated bottom bar; sticky sub-headers handle topic filter segments.

## Elevation & Depth
The system utilizes subtle tonal layering and low-contrast borders instead of heavy drop shadows, reinforcing an academic atmosphere focused on distraction-free content.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. Unbroken backdrop surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer):** `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px perimeter border of `#E2E8F0`. Soft ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Tooltips):** `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border and directional soft diffusion: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` elevated over a tinted backdrop veil (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

## Shapes
A roundedness tier of `2` provides an approachable, ergonomic feel while remaining grounded and modern. 

- Interactive controls (buttons, input boxes) utilize standard `0.5rem` (8px) corner curvature.
- Content units, lesson overview panels, and community containers adopt `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) corner radii to soften interface density.
- Badges, status pills, and avatars use fully circular geometries (`9999px`) to contrast cleanly against structural rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#4F46E5` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md` (8px). Subtle brightness shift (`#4338CA`) on hover. Height: 40px (md), 48px (lg).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#334155` text. On hover: `#F1F5F9` surface fill and `#0F172A` text.
- **Accent:** Filled `#06B6D4` with white text, utilized exclusively for real-time collaboration actions (e.g., "Join Live Study Room").

### Role Badges
Rendered using `label-sm` in `Inter`, all uppercase with `0.04em` letter spacing, `rounded-full` (9999px) with horizontal padding of `8px` and vertical padding of `2px`:
- **Guest:** `#F1F5F9` background with `#475569` text.
- **Member:** Soft cyan tint (`#ECFEFF` background, `#0E7490` text, 1px `#CFFAFE` border).
- **Moderator:** Amber authority tint (`#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, 1px `#FDE68A` border).

### Cards
- **Discussion / Study Card:** White surface, `rounded-xl` (24px), framed with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Internal padding: `1.5rem`. Contains a header row (author avatar, name, role badge, timestamp), clear content area (`body-md`), and a footer with count chips (replies, upvotes, study bookmarks).
- **Hover State:** Border transitions subtly to `#CBD5E1` with an elevated micro-offset (`translate-y: -1px`).

### Tabs & Segmented Controls
- Segmented controls feature a `#F1F5F9` pill container enclosing `rounded-lg` segment buttons.
- Active tab is rendered with a clean white background, `#0F172A` text, and a crisp micro-shadow, clearly distinguishing active discussion streams from general feeds.

### Input Fields & Search
- Inputs sit at 44px height, with a `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and `rounded-md` corners. Text uses `body-md` in `#0F172A`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state shifts border color to `#4F46E5` backed by an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)`).

### Modal Dialogs
- Centered on screen, constrained to 560px width, `rounded-xl` (24px) background in pure white with Level 3 elevation.
- Dedicated header section separated with a subtle `#F1F5F9` divider, containing title (`headline-sm`) and dismiss control.

### Empty States
- Composed within a dotted or dashed `#CBD5E1` border container.
- Uses a dual-tone circular illustration container in `#EEF2FF` with `#4F46E5` icons, flanked by a `title-md` header, a `body-sm` explanatory sentence, and a single primary action button (e.g., "Start First Discussion").
